# Changelog

## 0.2.0 (2026-11-01)

- Renames scientific outcomes to `supported`, `not_supported`, and `inconclusive`.
- Separates scientific interpretation from a later candidate acceptance or promotion decision.

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-01)

- Adds the scientific-method domain model.
- Adds questions, hypotheses, predictions, variables, controls, measurements, and observations.
- Adds machine-evaluable comparison, improvement, regression-limit, and composite criteria.
- Adds accepted, rejected, and inconclusive conclusions.
- Adds optional serde support for experiment frameworks.

## 0.0.1 (2026-11-01)

- Initial crate publication.
